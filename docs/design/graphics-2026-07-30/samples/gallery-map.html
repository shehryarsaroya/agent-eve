THE COMPACT · the map, seven ways

Seven treatments of one screen. The map that ships today is honest and legible and reads like a good technical diagram — it does not feel like a galaxy, and that is the whole gap. Every image below carries the same real world out of VISUAL-ASSET-BIBLE.md §§6–10 — 30 charted systems, 4 constellations, 35 lanes, 10 straits, tiers COMMONS 4 · MARCHES 18 · FRONTIER 8, the LODE as node weight and THE VERGE as one continuous solid outline — so what varies between them is treatment, not content. Judge the feeling; the facts are held constant on purpose.

The one thing every concept adds. Hundreds of faint uncharted background stars. The engine's 30 systems are a rules surface and cannot change, but a dense field of dim unnamed stars behind them makes the sky feel populated while the 30 that matter read as the charted ones — bright, ringed, labelled, joined by lanes and interactive, against dim and dead.

EVE’s look, our words. §3’s vocabulary is a rules surface in this project, so every prompt carries the same explicit ban list — ISK, nullsec, sovereignty, entosis, corporation, killboard and eighty more — and supplies the canon instead. Names come from the gazetteer only: an earlier sweep invented sys-31 and a handle morrow that collided with the constellation MARROW.

And every prompt carries a legibility contract, because A2 says legibility is the interface: whatever the atmosphere does, the three tiers, every claim tint, every strait mark, every unbroken VERGE outline and all 30 system labels must survive it. Where atmosphere and a label collide, the atmosphere yields.
